This website didn't exist until I made it. The photos on [/look](joelacosta.ai/look) didn't exist until I shot them. The deep conversations, shower playlists, and random infatuations we all have are all creations, yet we don't consider ourselves *creative* for some reason.\
\
**Everyone is a Creator** is the title of the first chapter of [Rick Rubin's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rick_Rubin) [*The Creative Act*](https://sites.prh.com/thecreativeact). This chapter, among many recent conversations i've had with many really interesting people, have led me to realize that being creative isn't a magical trait some people are born with and others aren't. A few months ago, someone  asked me if I had any creative hobbies, and I was genuinely stumped. We are all creative, this website is proof of that.\
\
This website serves 3 main purposes (and will expand over time):

1. To host a repository of, and share the things I work on, learn, and find interesting (for future or external reference).
2. To [build in public](https://www.paddle.com/blog/build-in-public-boost-your-engagement).
3. To create.

This website is living proof of me tapping into the creativity a version of myself three months ago would never have acknowledged. Creativity is within and all around us. It's a beautiful way to perceive the world around you, and yourself. You should try it.
